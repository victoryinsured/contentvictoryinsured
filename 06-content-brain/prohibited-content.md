# Prohibited Content

Generated content should not:

- Invent beliefs Victoria has never expressed.
- Sound like generic LinkedIn or motivational-guru content.
- Copy another creator's wording or identity.
- Make unsupported income or employment guarantees.
- Flatten nuanced career advice into absolute rules.
- Give away proprietary mentorship methodology when the content strategy says not to.
- Use fake client stories or fabricated results.

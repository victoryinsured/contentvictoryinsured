# Victory Daily Content Programming — v1

## Goal

Default target: 5 posts per day.

The five posts should NOT be five unrelated ideas and should NOT be five versions of the same idea.

Each duration has a different strategic job.

# FULL 5-POST DAY

## SLOT 1 — 7 Seconds
Primary job:
**FYP discovery / trend participation / instant curiosity**

Best formats:
- trending sound
- reaction
- transformation
- one-line realization
- false-choice reframe
- money/opportunity
- identity

Rules:
- one idea only
- sound + text must work together
- do not teach the lesson
- viewer should understand the idea immediately
- grid headline still matters

Question it answers:
**Why should a stranger stop on Victoria?**

---

## SLOT 2 — 15 Seconds
Primary job:
**Punchy perspective / micro-teaching / opinion**

Best formats:
- "stop doing this"
- quick correction
- one surprising fact
- one career realization
- one claims myth
- response to a comment
- fast money/career contrast

Structure:
HOOK
→ ONE POINT
→ PAYOFF

Rules:
- no list of five things
- no long explanation
- should feel like Victoria caught the viewer in the hallway and told them something important

Question it answers:
**What can Victoria make me realize fast?**

---

## SLOT 3 — 30 Seconds
Primary job:
**Useful takeaway / compact teaching**

Best formats:
- one problem → one solution
- one role explanation
- one strategy
- one credential explanation
- one audience comment
- one career positioning lesson
- one "here's what I would do"

Structure:
PROBLEM
→ EXPLANATION
→ EXAMPLE
→ NEXT STEP

Question it answers:
**What can I use from Victoria today?**

---

## SLOT 4 — ~60 Seconds
Primary job:
**Search / evergreen authority**

Best formats:
- searchable question
- how-to
- license/certification
- salary
- getting hired
- job market
- role breakdown
- beginner roadmap

Structure:
EXACT QUERY
→ CLEAR PROMISE
→ STEPS
→ NUANCE
→ TAKEAWAY

Question it answers:
**What is Victoria qualified to teach me?**

---

## SLOT 5 — ~60 Seconds
Primary job:
**Personality / authority / series / story / conversion**

Rotate between:
- Claims Corner
- personal story
- strong opinion
- experienced-adjuster strategy
- career positioning
- faith when authentic
- behind-the-scenes
- webinar / offer bridge when appropriate

This is intentionally different from Slot 4.

Slot 4 = answer existing demand.
Slot 5 = deepen relationship / create new demand / build Victoria's world.

Question it answers:
**Why do I want to keep following Victoria specifically?**

# DAILY COHERENCE RULE

Posts may share a strategic world without repeating the same script.

Example theme:
"experienced adjusters are stuck"

7 sec:
reaction to "nobody is hiring"

15 sec:
"Your experience isn't automatically the problem."

30 sec:
one reason experienced adjusters stay stuck

60 sec Search:
"Why can't I find a claims job with 6 years of experience?"

60 sec authority:
Claims Corner / role expansion into specialty claims

This creates a cluster, not duplication.

---

# DURATION IS NOT DISTRIBUTION

Victoria's historical data shows:
- ultra-short content frequently receives heavy FYP distribution
- long-form can ALSO receive heavy FYP distribution
- long-form can be Search-led
- some long-form is hybrid

Therefore duration controls the CREATIVE JOB, not an assumed traffic source.

---

# ROTATION RULE

Across a week, rotate:
- beginner
- newly licensed
- experienced/stuck
- career expansion
- technical claims
- career strategy
- personal/faith
- market/current issue
- trend/culture
- conversion

Do not let beginner "how to become an adjuster" content take over the account.

---

# DAILY OUTPUT PACKAGE

For every slot output:
- duration
- content job
- audience
- topic
- hook
- ready-to-record script
- on-screen text
- grid title
- caption
- hashtags
- CTA if appropriate
- source of idea (audience comment / search / Victoria database / trend / market / creator pattern)
- why this slot fits the duration

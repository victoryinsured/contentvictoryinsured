# Brand Positioning Guardrails

## Claims Positioning

Victory does NOT position catastrophe claims work as the desired end-state.

### Core positioning
Victory is a blue-ocean career strategy brand focused on helping people find stronger, more stable, higher-value paths across the claims and insurance ecosystem.

### CAT work
CAT work may be referenced because it is part of Victoria's lived experience and industry background, but it should be used carefully.

Use CAT primarily as:
- historical context
- contrast
- a lesson
- a comparison point
- evidence of what Victoria learned about the industry

Do NOT:
- present catastrophe work as the primary recommendation
- make the content brain default to CAT-adjuster examples
- imply that chasing storms is the preferred long-term strategy
- frame instability as aspirational

### Tone when contrasting CAT work
Classy shade, not bitterness.

Approved positioning themes:
- stability over chasing storms
- career architecture over deployment dependency
- broader claims ecosystems over one narrow path
- durable professional capital over temporary spikes in income
- strategic specialization over reactive work

## Sound / Culture Preference

Victoria prefers sounds that feel culturally native to her taste and audience.

### Preferred
- urban / Black-culture sounds
- sounds Victoria naturally likes and would actually use
- spoken-word trends that create a setup for on-screen text
- sounds common in the parts of TikTok Victoria actually watches
- culturally relevant reaction, humor, confidence, gratitude, flex, reflection, or transformation sounds

### Avoid
- forcing generic mainstream trend sounds that do not feel like Victoria
- using a sound just because a trend dashboard says it is popular
- recommending trends that feel culturally off-brand

## Rule
Trend selection must optimize for Victoria-fit, not raw trend rank.

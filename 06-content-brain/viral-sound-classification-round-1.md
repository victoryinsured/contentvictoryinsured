# Viral Sound Classification — Round 1

This file classifies the sounds Victoria already supplied. The goal is to determine whether each winning post benefited from a broad trend, a trend format, a popular song, a creator/original sound, or primarily Victoria's own concept.

## Classification labels

- **Verified Broad Trend** — strong external evidence the audio/song had substantial TikTok adoption.
- **Trend-Format / FYP Confirmed** — Victoria observed repeated use / recognizable format on TikTok, but public web data is insufficient to independently quantify it.
- **Creator / Original Sound** — creator-originated audio that may spread, but is not automatically a broad music trend.
- **Personal-Taste Sound** — Victoria liked the sound; trend status not established.
- **Pending** — not enough evidence yet.

# Preliminary finding

Victoria's winners do NOT appear to require one single sound type.

Winning posts include:
1. creator/original sounds,
2. FYP/trend-format original sounds,
3. culturally familiar edited audio,
4. broadly popular TikTok songs.

Therefore the Content Brain should NOT use:
"Find the biggest trending song."

It should use:
"Find a sound with the strongest combination of current momentum + Victoria taste + community grammar + niche-adaptation potential."

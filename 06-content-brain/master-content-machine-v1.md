# Victory Content Machine — Master Script Engine v1

## Purpose

The Content Machine should reduce Victoria's daily content work to:

1. receive content brief
2. generate ready-to-record content
3. Victoria records
4. move on

The system should NOT make Victoria brainstorm from scratch.

It should decide:
- what content job is needed
- which engine fits
- which hook type fits
- how much explanation is appropriate
- how to sound like Victoria
- what caption/hashtags/grid text are needed

---

# STEP 1 — Choose the content JOB

Every post must have ONE primary job.

## A. FYP Discovery
Best for:
- reaching new people
- curiosity
- broad human/emotional entry
- money/opportunity
- identity
- transformation
- trends

Usually:
- 7–15 sec
- or story-led talking video

## B. Search Discovery
Best for:
- exact audience questions
- how-to
- license/certification questions
- step-by-step
- job-specific questions
- long-tail evergreen traffic

Usually:
- 30–90 sec
- direct teaching

## C. Authority / Saveable Teaching
Best for:
- Claims Corner
- technical explanations
- career strategy
- role breakdowns
- frameworks
- checklists

Usually:
- 45–120 sec
- series-friendly

## D. Trust / Story
Best for:
- Victoria origin story
- struggle → outcome
- single motherhood
- faith
- career lessons
- mistakes
- "what I wish I knew"

## E. Opinion / Correction
Best for:
- myths
- bad claims advice
- CAT assumptions
- career misconceptions
- "stop doing this"
- contrarian but useful takes

## F. Conversion
Best for:
- webinar
- guide
- mentorship
- campaign
- offer

Must still deliver value before CTA.

# STEP 2 — Choose the ENGINE

## ENGINE 1 — 7-Second Opportunity Reaction

Use when:
- opportunity is instantly understandable
- money/result is concrete
- claims anchor can fit in one sentence

Formula:
SPECIFIC OPPORTUNITY
→ SPECIFIC MONEY
→ CLAIMS ANCHOR
→ SOUND/REACTION COMPLETES THE IDEA

Example structure:
"When you're offered $4,000 a week to process claims."

Job:
FYP discovery + follower acquisition.

---

## ENGINE 2 — 7-Second Transformation

Formula:
BEFORE STATE
→ FAITH / TURNING POINT
→ CONCRETE AFTER STATE
→ OPTIONAL CLAIMS ANCHOR

Use for:
- faith
- homelessness → career
- single motherhood
- financial transformation

Job:
emotional resonance + discovery.

---

## ENGINE 3 — 7-Second Identity Bridge

Formula:
IDENTITY
→ STRUGGLE
→ TRANSFORMATION
→ CLAIMS / INCOME RESULT

Example:
"POV: single mother trying to make a way and BOOM God did it."

Job:
trust + follower conversion + qualified identity connection.

---

## ENGINE 4 — 7-Second Cultural Trend Reframe

Formula:
CURRENT SOUND / COMMUNITY GRAMMAR
→ RELATABLE SETUP
→ MONEY / CAREER / CLAIMS REFRAME

Rule:
The on-screen text must COMPLETE the sound.

If the text could work unchanged over any random audio, reject it.

Use:
- creator sounds
- Black/urban cultural trends
- spoken audio
- humorous justification / rebuttal / reaction
- financial independence

---

## ENGINE 5 — Searchable How-To

Formula:
EXACT QUESTION / PROBLEM
→ DIRECT PROMISE
→ STEP 1
→ STEP 2
→ STEP 3
→ IMPORTANT WARNING / NUANCE
→ NEXT ACTION

Victoria examples:
- licenses
- certifications
- rosters
- how to get started
- reciprocity
- salary
- specialty claims

Job:
Search + saves + followers.

---

## ENGINE 6 — "If I Had to Start Over"

Formula:
HIGH-STAKES SITUATION
→ SPECIFIC RESULT
→ "HERE'S EXACTLY WHAT I WOULD DO"
→ STEP-BY-STEP
→ ACTION REFRAME

Victoria example:
"If I lost everything and had to start over again as a new claims adjuster..."

Job:
Search + authority + saveability.

---

## ENGINE 7 — FYP Story

Formula:
BROAD HUMAN HOOK
→ PERSONALITY / HUMOR
→ TENSION
→ STORY
→ CLAIMS REVEAL
→ MONEY / TRANSFORMATION PAYOFF

Victoria example:
"You ever been so broke that when someone tells you how to make some money you don't even believe them?"

Rule:
Do NOT lead with technical claims language.

Job:
new audience + personality + followers.

---

## ENGINE 8 — Hybrid Aspirational Teaching

Formula:
DESIRE / MONEY
→ "I DON'T KNOW WHERE TO START"
→ FIRST STEP
→ TECHNICAL EXPLANATION
→ SIMPLIFY
→ RECAP

Victoria example:
"So you wanna be a claims adjuster and make $4,000 a week..."

Job:
FYP + Search.

---

## ENGINE 9 — Technical Explainer / Claims Corner

Formula:
TOPIC
→ WHO THIS IS FOR
→ WHY IT MATTERS
→ EXPLAIN IN NORMAL LANGUAGE
→ EXAMPLE
→ WHAT PEOPLE GET WRONG
→ TAKEAWAY

Rule:
Technical does NOT mean formal.

Use Victoria language:
- "okay"
- "boom"
- "girl"
- "baby"
- "in a nutshell"
- "what people don't tell you"

Job:
authority + saves + series consistency.

---

## ENGINE 10 — Myth / Opinion Correction

Formula:
COMMON BELIEF
→ VICTORIA REACTION
→ WHY IT'S WRONG / INCOMPLETE
→ BETTER FRAME
→ WHAT TO DO INSTEAD

Use for:
- CAT work
- "claims adjuster is one job"
- "there are no jobs"
- "I have no experience"
- "just apply on Indeed"
- "license = job"

Tone:
confident, conversational, classy shade.

---

## ENGINE 11 — False-Choice Reframe

Formula:
WHAT PEOPLE THINK THEY HAVE TO DO
→ WHAT VICTORIA KNOWS
→ BETTER POSSIBILITY

Example:
"Me explaining that you can make $4,000 a week working insurance claims without chasing storms, climbing roofs or becoming a salesman."

Job:
trend adaptation + positioning.

# STEP 5 — Content rules learned from Victoria's data

## Rule 1
7-second content usually creates:
- possibility
- identity
- contrast
- curiosity

It does NOT need to teach the whole lesson.

## Rule 2
Long-form talking videos can be:
- FYP-led
- Search-led
- hybrid

Do not assume:
SHORT = FYP
LONG = Search

## Rule 3
Search-led videos usually begin with:
EXACT PROBLEM / EXACT AUDIENCE / EXACT PROMISE

## Rule 4
FYP-led talking videos often begin with:
BROAD HUMAN EMOTION / MONEY / STORY / IDENTITY

Then reveal claims.

## Rule 5
Specific money performs well when truthful and contextually appropriate.

Examples:
- $4,000/week
- $10,000/month
- six figures

Do not use money randomly if the topic does not support it.

## Rule 6
Utility matters heavily.

Victoria's audience saves:
- steps
- credentials
- lists
- explanations
- "what to do next"

## Rule 7
A video can succeed even with imperfect completion rate if:
- Search traffic is strong
- saves are high
- follower conversion is strong
- viewer intent is high

## Rule 8
Faith is authentic to Victoria and may stand alone.

Do not force faith into claims content.

## Rule 9
Victory does NOT glorify catastrophe work.

CAT may be:
- history
- contrast
- misconception
- classy shade

Do not position chasing storms as the desired career end state.

## Rule 10
Victory helps people make more money from stronger career positioning.

Do not normalize:
- working two jobs to survive
- random side hustles as the brand solution
- instability as success

# STEP 8 — Repetition control

The system must not generate the same idea in different clothes every day.

Track:
- topic
- engine
- hook type
- audience maturity
- emotional trigger
- CTA
- sound type

Do not repeat the same combination too frequently.

Example:
Do NOT produce 5 versions of:
"Claims pays $4,000/week."

Instead rotate:
- opportunity
- myth
- story
- technical
- audience question
- specialty role
- identity
- objection
- search FAQ
- trend reframe

---

# STEP 9 — Daily Content Machine Logic

A suggested daily mix:

1. one FYP/discovery piece
2. one Search/teaching piece
3. one authority/series piece OR story/opinion piece

The system should decide based on:
- recent posts
- audience questions
- saved/favorite trends
- current market/news
- creator research
- performance history
- what has not been covered recently

The objective is not maximum posting volume.

The objective is:
**qualified discovery + searchable authority + human trust + commercial relevance.**

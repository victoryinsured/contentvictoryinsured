# Sound Discovery Source Priority

## Goal
Find sounds that are:
1. culturally aligned with Victoria,
2. actually being used now,
3. understandable enough to pair with strong on-screen text,
4. relevant to Victory's audience,
5. not simply whatever TikTok's generic trend dashboard says is popular.

## Source priority

### 1. Victoria's TikTok Favorite Sounds
Highest-value taste signal.

Use Victoria's exported Favorite Sounds history to learn:
- which sounds she naturally saves
- recurring cultural/music patterns
- creator sounds vs songs
- spoken-word vs music-only preference
- emotional/comedic styles she gravitates toward

### 2. Victoria's Favorite / Saved Videos
Use saved videos to learn:
- creators Victoria likes
- how sounds are paired with text
- trend mechanics
- humor style
- pacing
- on-screen copy
- cultural fit

### 3. Current creator/original sounds
Actively search creator sounds that are beginning to repeat across Victoria-aligned feeds and creators.

Prioritize:
- Black/urban creator ecosystems
- spoken-word sounds
- humor/reaction audio
- culturally familiar clips
- sounds that offer a clear text-completion mechanic

### 4. Current viral/trending sounds
Use broader TikTok trend data as one signal, not the decision-maker.

A sound must still pass:
- Victoria taste
- cultural fit
- brand fit
- sound/text mechanic fit
- niche adaptation potential

### 5. Victoria manual trend inbox
Victoria can manually submit sounds she keeps seeing on her FYP.

Manual submissions receive elevated consideration because her FYP is a direct taste/audience signal.

## Required scoring fields

Each candidate sound should be scored on:
- trend momentum
- creator/original-sound momentum
- Victoria taste match
- Black/urban cultural fit
- spoken-word usefulness
- community grammar clarity
- brand appropriateness
- claims/career adaptation potential
- faith adaptation potential
- humor potential
- saturation risk
- shelf-life estimate

## Final decision rule
Do NOT recommend a sound merely because it is popular.

Recommend when:
TREND / CREATOR MOMENTUM + VICTORIA TASTE + CULTURAL FIT + CLEAR COMMUNITY GRAMMAR + STRONG NICHE ADAPTATION = HIGH

If the screen text cannot naturally complete the sound, skip it.

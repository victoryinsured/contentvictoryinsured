# Sound Origin + Community Grammar

## Why origin matters
A trending sound should not be interpreted only from its literal words. The original scene/context can create subtext that survives when TikTok users reuse it.

The Content Brain should store three distinct layers:

1. **Original context** — what was happening when the audio was first said/created.
2. **Literal audio meaning** — what the words say on their face.
3. **Community grammar** — the pattern/joke creators have collectively built around the sound.

## Example: "Two Fishes"

### Original context
The audio came from a Love Island situation in which a man was presenting himself as caring deeply for a woman while also behaving like a player. His "I love two fish" explanation functions as a rationalization for wanting two romantic options.

### Literal meaning
Why settle for one when you can have two?

### Original subtext
- rationalization
- player logic
- obvious excuse delivered as though it makes sense
- self-serving explanation
- "technically, hear me out..."

### Community grammar
Creators can reuse the sound for:
- humorously defending questionable behavior
- biased defense of someone they love
- explaining why two things can coexist
- rejecting a false either/or
- giving an intentionally overconfident justification
- informational "you don't actually have to choose" reframes

### Important rule
Do NOT reduce the sound to one formula.

A sound can support multiple valid modes if the original subtext and current community usage allow them.

Potential routes:
- humorous_justification
- false_choice_reframe
- biased_defense
- informational_both_can_be_true

The router should choose based on:
1. the specific current usage pattern,
2. Victoria's intended message,
3. brand fit,
4. whether the on-screen text completes the sound's logic.

## Core lesson
The computer should understand both WHERE THE SOUND CAME FROM and WHAT TIKTOK HAS TURNED IT INTO.

Origin explains subtext.
Community usage explains current grammar.
Victoria training determines how that grammar should be adapted for Victory.

## Emerging rule: Trend Adaptation
Victoria does not chase views for their own sake.

### Required adaptation rule
If the trend is not faith-led content, the post should connect back to at least one of:
- claims
- career mobility
- financial independence
- income
- professional transformation
- career strategy
- Victoria's lived claims/career story

### Preferred creative method
TREND / SOUND → identify emotional meaning → translate into Victoria's world → add a concrete niche-specific payoff.

### Strong examples
- dance trend → financial independence from a claims income
- gratitude sound → thank younger self for hard claims work that built current career
- POV sound → leap of faith into claims → $260K/year
- transformation sound → homelessness → $4K/week claims career

## Key distinction
The sound may improve discovery or native feel, but the content must still carry Victoria's positioning. If the trend goes viral but does not strengthen audience fit, it is not strategically successful.

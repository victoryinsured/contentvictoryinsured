# Preliminary Winning Patterns — TikTok

Status: Early pattern analysis. Update after transcripts/topics are reviewed.

## Primary objective: Qualified virality
The goal is not generic reach. The goal is for TikTok to correctly identify the intended audience, then earn strong watch, save, share, comment, and follow behavior from that audience.

## Strongest early signal: utility / reference value
Across the currently logged high-performing sample, saves are unusually strong relative to likes.

Early sample observations:
- Median saves are roughly 42% of likes.
- 9 of 14 measured videos have saves equal to at least 40% of likes.
- 5 of 14 have saves equal to at least 70% of likes.
- Video #5 (step-by-step: how to get started as a claims adjuster) had 13.4K saves vs 11.6K likes.
- The 10-minute video had 3,016 saves vs 2,835 likes.
- Video #9 had 5,215 saves vs 5,807 likes.

Interpretation: Victoria's audience frequently treats her content as a resource, not just entertainment.

## Formula implications

### 1. Solve a high-intent problem
Prioritize questions viewers are actively trying to solve:
- How do I get into this?
- What should I do first?
- What job should I target?
- What am I doing wrong?
- How does this industry actually work?

### 2. Make the content worth saving
Include:
- steps
- lists
- sequences
- requirements
- decision rules
- examples
- job titles
- salary/context
- mistakes to avoid

### 3. Make it worth sending
Build moments where the viewer naturally thinks:
- "My friend needs this."
- "This explains what I was trying to tell someone."
- "I know someone trying to get into this."
- "Nobody told us this."

### 4. Lead with specificity
The opening should identify the person/problem/topic quickly rather than using a generic motivational hook.

### 5. Teach through Victoria's lived experience
Victoria's first viral video indicates autobiography + discovery + specific money + obstacles + lesson can carry educational information without feeling like a lecture.

### 6. Keep natural Victoria commentary
Do not over-script away side comments, reactions, humor, disbelief, or conversational language. Authority should feel lived-in rather than corporate.

### 7. Use explicit topic language for audience classification
The spoken opening, on-screen text, caption, and relevant hashtags should make the topic and intended audience obvious (claims adjuster, insurance claims, career change, etc.).

## Working content formula

RIGHT PERSON → SPECIFIC PROBLEM/DESIRE → CURIOSITY/TENSION → CONCRETE VALUE → VICTORIA EXPLANATION/EXAMPLE → SAVE/SHARE-WORTHY TAKEAWAY

Alternative utility formula:

SEARCHABLE QUESTION → DIRECT ANSWER → STEP 1/2/3 → IMPORTANT WARNING/NUANCE → NEXT ACTION

## Do not conclude yet
We still need transcripts/topics for the strongest videos before locking the final formula. Metrics show what behavior the content generated; transcripts will reveal what creative elements caused that behavior.

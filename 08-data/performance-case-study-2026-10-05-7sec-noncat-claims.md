## Comment signal
User reports that the comments overwhelmingly repeated "more info."

Interpretation: the short-form belief disruption created an information gap. Viewers were not simply reacting; they wanted the missing explanation.

## Content-machine lesson
Do NOT merely duplicate "$4,000/week without roofs."

Preserve the mechanism and rotate the subject:
1. Existing belief / mental model
2. Contradiction or hidden possibility
3. Leave enough information gap to trigger "wait, how?"
4. Longer follow-up content explains the mechanism.

Examples:
- Nurse → hidden claims/career functions
- Customer service → functions already used in claims
- Claims adjuster → more than one job/claims lane
- Experienced professional → career change does not necessarily mean starting over
- Credential seeker → credential may not be the actual gap

## Victoria content fingerprint
Expose career possibilities people cannot see because they are looking at their experience, the market, or claims through the wrong mental model.

Recurring desired reaction:
"I didn't realize my experience/career/claims could do THAT."

## Important execution note
Distribution and audience fit were strong, but retention dropped sharply at 0:01. Future 7-second tests should preserve the belief-disruption concept while testing stronger first-frame readability, curiosity, looping, and completion mechanics.

# Audience Comment Demand — October 2026

Source: TikTok Studio recent comments supplied by Victoria.

## Major recurring audience problems

### 1. Licensed but still cannot find a good job
Real audience language:
- "I’ve been a claims adjuster for 6 years and can’t find a decent job anywhere"
- "I’ve had my license for 4 years. Worked for Geico for 3 years… can’t find another job in this career"

Strategic meaning:
This is a major content lane beyond beginner licensing.

Audience need:
**I already have experience. Why am I still stuck?**

Potential content series:
- Licensed But Still Stuck
- Why Experienced Adjusters Still Can’t Get Hired
- What To Do When Your Claims Experience Isn’t Converting
- Stop Applying to the Same Claims Jobs

## Core strategic finding

Victoria's audience is not one-stage.

There are at least four career maturity groups:

1. Curious outsider — "How do I get started?"
2. Newly licensed — "What do I do now?"
3. Experienced but stuck — "Why can’t I get a better job?"
4. Career-expansion seeker — "What other claims specialties exist?"

The content system should generate content for all four rather than over-serving beginners.

## Hook-language rule

Whenever possible, borrow the audience's natural phrasing instead of replacing it with corporate language.

Example:
BAD:
"Professionals experiencing career stagnation in claims"

BETTER:
"I’ve been an adjuster for 6 years and still can’t find a decent claims job."

This language should feed:
- hooks
- comment-response videos
- FAQ series
- Search content
- Claims Corner
- long-form teaching

## Finding 1: Talking videos are NOT one distribution type

There are at least two major long-form engines.

### Engine A — Searchable Evergreen Teaching
Examples:
- #5: 86.6% Search
- #11: 79.5% Search

Characteristics:
- clear claims topic
- direct educational value
- high-intent questions
- strong saves
- long-tail discoverability
- follower acquisition from usefulness

### Engine B — FYP Talking Content
Examples:
- #6: 90.6% FYP
- #10: 90.0% FYP

Characteristics:
- TikTok pushes the talking video directly through For You
- long form does NOT require Search to perform
- personality, story, framing, hook, identity, opinion, or emotional relevance may be doing more work than keyword demand

### Engine C — Hybrid Education
Example:
- #12 original: 70.8% FYP + 25.7% Search

This may be strategically valuable because it can receive broad discovery while still indexing for a high-intent topic.

## Finding 2: #6 is currently the standout follower-conversion long-form post

## Finding 4: Reposting the same content does not reproduce the same distribution

#12 original vs #12 repost:
- Search: 25.7% → 0.1%
- FYP: 70.8% → 97.1%
- Avg watch: 19.8s → 15.06s
- Completion: 11.8% → 7.5%
- Follower conversion: ~0.96% → ~0.70%

The content stayed useful (save rate remained ~4.4%), but discovery routing changed.

Therefore:
**content quality and distribution source must be analyzed separately.**

## Finding 5: Long-form can absolutely be pushed by FYP

The short-form hypothesis was:
7-second videos are primarily FYP discovery.

That is true in the current short-form sample.

However, long-form videos can ALSO be primarily FYP-distributed:
- #6: 90.6% FYP
- #10: 90.0% FYP
- #12 repost: 97.1% FYP

So the strategic split is not:
SHORT = FYP
LONG = Search

The more accurate model is:
- some short-form posts are FYP discovery
- some long-form posts are FYP discovery
- some long-form posts are Search discovery
- some posts are hybrid

The next analytical question is:
**what creative characteristics cause TikTok to route Victoria's talking videos toward FYP vs Search?**

## Finding 6: Account-level data supports a multi-stage ecosystem

Last 365 days:
- FYP: 67%
- Search: 24.1%
- Personal profile: 8.3%

This suggests Victoria has three meaningful surfaces:
1. FYP discovery
2. Search discovery
3. Profile consumption

But current analytics do NOT prove a causal path such as:
7-second post → profile → long-form post.

That remains a hypothesis to test.

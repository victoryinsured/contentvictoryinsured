## Key findings

### 1. This was an FYP-led discovery video
98.2% of traffic came from For You.

This is a fundamentally different distribution engine from Video #5, whose traffic was dominated by Search.

### 2. The sound page itself was not the traffic source
Sound contributed <0.1% of traffic.

Important distinction:
A trend/sound can still improve the creative format, native feel, pacing, or viewer response even when viewers do not arrive through the Sound page.

Do NOT interpret Sound traffic <0.1% as proof the audio was irrelevant creatively. It only shows the TikTok sound page was not a meaningful direct acquisition source.

### 3. The content still communicated "claims" clearly
Even though the caption only said "Thank you God for his favor. ✨ #fyp," the small amount of Search traffic included claims-specific queries such as "claims adjuster salary."

This suggests the on-screen text / content itself gave TikTok enough topic context to associate the video with claims-adjuster content.

### 4. The short format appears replayable
Average watch time exceeded the nominal video length.

This supports testing loop/replay behavior as a key metric for 7-second/ultra-short content.

### 5. Transformation + faith + specific income is a viable discovery combination
The creative combines:
BEFORE STATE → GOD / TRANSFORMATION → SPECIFIC AFTER STATE → CLAIMS NICHE

The viewer can understand the emotional and career transformation without a long explanation.

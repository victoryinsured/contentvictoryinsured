# Approved Trend Adaptation — "Two Fishes"

## On-screen text
"Me explaining that you can make $4,000 a week without chasing the storms and climbing roofs."

## Why Victoria approved it
This matches the underlying trend mechanic: people act as though two desirable outcomes are mutually exclusive, while the audio argues that you can have both.

In this adaptation:
- desirable outcome 1 = high income
- desirable outcome 2 = stability / no storm-chasing / no roof climbing

## Why it fits Victory
- Reinforces the blue-ocean positioning
- Uses CAT/field work as classy contrast rather than aspiration
- Makes a clear career point while still fitting the joke
- Niche-specific enough for claims professionals
- Broad enough that non-claims viewers can still understand the tension between money and stability

## Trend logic
FALSE EITHER/OR → WHY NOT BOTH?

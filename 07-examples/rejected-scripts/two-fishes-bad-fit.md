# Rejected Trend Adaptation — "Two Fishes"

## Rejected idea
Use the trend to justify having two claims jobs / two contracts at once.

## Why Victoria rejected it
This contradicts Victory's positioning.

Victory helps people build stronger, higher-value, often six-figure career paths. The brand should not imply that the audience needs two jobs to make enough money or that juggling jobs is the desired outcome.

## Strategic lesson
A trend adaptation can be mechanically correct and still be brand-wrong.

The Content Brain must check:
1. Does the joke fit the sound?
2. Does the joke fit Victoria's voice?
3. Does the underlying message support Victory's positioning?
4. Does it accidentally teach or normalize something Victoria would not recommend?

## New guardrail
Do not use:
- second-job humor
- multiple-job survival framing
- "stack jobs to make enough money" positioning

unless Victoria explicitly approves a specific context.

## Important insight
The hardest part is not finding a trend. It is finding a Victoria-specific situation that:
- closes the audio's joke,
- feels natural,
- is actually funny,
- stays on-brand,
- and still reinforces the desired career narrative.

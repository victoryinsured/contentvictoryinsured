# Topic Pillars

## Content mission
Helping people understand how their existing experience translates into higher-value careers—especially inside the claims/insurance ecosystem—and teaching them how to position, compete, get paid for what they already know, and crush the competition.

## Initial content territories

### 1. You Already Have Experience
Show people the hidden business value inside work they underestimate.

### 2. You Don't Know What Jobs Exist
Expose overlooked roles, claims niches, ecosystems, and career connections.

### 3. Here's Why You're Not Getting Hired
Positioning, targeting, interviews, resumes, competency gaps, and application strategy.

### 4. How Victoria Thinks About Careers
Victoria's frameworks, opinions, contrarian takes, and decision logic.

### 5. Proof + Founder Authority
Client transformations, Victoria's career story, business-building insights, job-posting breakdowns, and real examples.

# Shelby Video Analysis — Sales Maxxing 02

## Why Victoria likely likes this video

This video is built as a rapid sequence of compact, high-conviction principles. It feels dense, useful, memorable, and psychologically smart without becoming formal.

## 1. Every line can stand alone

Examples:
- "Sales is 20% the words you say and 80% your confidence and conviction."
- "People don't buy because they understand you, they buy because they feel understood."
- "Whoever asks the questions controls the conversation."
- "People buy with emotion, but justify with logic."
- "Don't pitch until you fully understand their problem."
- "After you say price, shut up."

Mechanic:
**ONE-LINE PRINCIPLE STACKING**

Each sentence is independently useful.

This creates constant reward for the viewer.

## 2. It uses contrast constantly

Examples:
- words vs confidence
- understand you vs feel understood
- young vs older
- talk 80 / talk 20
- push vs pull
- emotion vs logic
- where they are vs where they want to be
- first objection vs real objection

Mechanic:
**A vs B**

Contrast makes abstract ideas easier to understand and remember.

Victoria's content can use:

# Shelby Formula Extracted From This Video

## Core Formula

**STRONG RULE**
→ **CONTRAST**
→ **ANOTHER RULE**
→ **EXAMPLE / SEGMENTATION**
→ **DIRECT COMMAND**
→ **PSYCHOLOGICAL INSIGHT**
→ **REFRAME**
→ **MEMORABLE CLOSE**

## Micro-lesson format

Each line often follows one of these shapes:

1. **X is not Y; X is Z**
2. **A matters less than B**
3. **People do X because Y**
4. **Stop doing X**
5. **Do X before Y**
6. **What looks like X is actually Y**
7. **If X happens, it means Y**
8. **After X, do Y**

These can become reusable script sentence patterns for Victory.

# Why This Matters for Victoria's Machine

This format is ideal when Victoria knows the subject deeply and has many strong opinions.

This format can also generate clips:
one 60-second stacked-principle video

# Formula Extracted

## Aspirational Proof-to-Utility Formula

**RESULT / FLEX**
→ **PERSONALITY / CONTEXT**
→ **SPECIFIC AUDIENCE**
→ **NUMBERED TACTICAL PROMISE**
→ **STEP 1 + WHY**
→ **STEP 2 + WHY**
→ **STEP 3 + WHY**
→ **REAL PROOF / EXAMPLE**
→ **SAVE-WORTHY CTA**

## Why this works for Victoria

This format is ideal for:
- salary/career outcomes
- "how I did it"
- interview strategy
- negotiating salary
- choosing employers
- claims career leverage
- licenses/certifications
- moving from CAT to stable work
- specialty claims
- career positioning

## Key principle

**Don't flex unless the flex teaches.**

If Victoria shows:
- money
- freedom
- career outcome
- stability
- lifestyle

the content should quickly answer:
**"What can the viewer learn from this?"**

That keeps aspiration aligned with trust and utility.
